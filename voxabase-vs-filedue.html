Voxabase vs FileDue

Both keep your files locked until the client pays. FileDue is a pay link for a single delivery. Voxabase is a branded delivery page for your business: the files, the invoice, the client’s approval and your own domain, in one link.

Which one fits

Choose Voxabase if…

  • You deliver for clients regularly and want it to look like your studio, not a payment link.
  • You want clients to approve the work or ask for changes in the same place.
  • You work with teammates, or want portals on your own domain.
  • You’d rather pay a flat monthly price and keep 100% of each invoice.

FileDue may suit you better if…

  • You deliver only now and then, and prefer a fee per sale to a subscription.
  • You want watermarked previews of the files before the client pays.
