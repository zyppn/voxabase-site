Voxabase vs Assembly

Assembly (formerly Copilot) is an all-in-one client workspace: a CRM, messaging, billing and an app builder, with a portal clients sign in to. Voxabase does one job: hand over finished work and get paid. Clients open one link, pay the invoice, and the files unlock.

Which one fits

Choose Voxabase if…

  • You mainly need to deliver files professionally and get paid for them.
  • You don’t want clients to create accounts or learn a new tool.
  • You want your own domain and no vendor branding for $39 a month, not $99.
  • You want to start free and stay under $50 a month.

Voxabase isn’t the right fit if…

  • You need a full CRM and client messaging in one place.
  • You rely on automations or an API to connect your tools.
  • You need HIPAA compliance or enforced two-factor sign-in for clients.
