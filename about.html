About Voxabase

The team behind the handoff.

Voxabase is built by four students from LSU, Ole Miss, Tulane and Auburn. Each of us owns one part of the business, from the code that delivers your files to the inbox that answers your questions.

The team

Small on purpose. Owned end to end.

No handoffs between departments. When something needs fixing, the person who owns it is the person who fixes it.

    Jacob Phillips

    ISDS · Louisiana State University

    • Ships core features from concept to production, across the frontend (React, Next.js, UI) and the backend (Node, databases, APIs).
    • Maintains and optimizes our Stripe integrations and file delivery pipelines, and keeps client portal links secure and encrypted.

  2. 02Content Strategist

    Tristan Kurtz

    Finance · University of Mississippi

    • Owns our brand voice and drives organic user acquisition, reaching out to potential clients and businesses.

  3. 03Customer Specialist

    Michael Guruli

    Cell and Molecular Biology · Tulane University

    • Owns the user experience and makes sure everyone using the platform has a smooth, hassle-free experience.

  4. 04Financial Analyst

    Chase Fernandez

    Finance · Auburn University

    • Analyzes competitor pricing tiers, tests subscription packages, and pins down exactly what turns a free account into a paid one.
